Unique Technological Advantage

⚡

eBPF Telemetry (Zero-Overhead)

Process observability directly at the OS kernel level. No noticeable CPU load (under 1%) and absolute resistance to masking (user-mode rootkits).

🧠

Magnesium MPC & Talus AI

Zero-Day attack verification using MPC cryptography. Your network traffic is never sent in plain-text to provider servers, unlike traditional EDRs.

⛓️

Blockchain Sui (Decentralized C2)

Replacing central C2 (Command & Control) servers with smart contracts. Total infrastructure resistance to hacker C2 takeovers and single points of failure.

🧊

Walrus Forensics Storage

Immutable, distributed evidence storage (memory dumps, FIM event logs). Guaranteed chain of custody for auditors, NIS2 requirements, and legal proceedings.

🔑

Multi-Sig Authorization

Protection against human error or admin account theft. Critical actions (e.g., global host isolation) require hardware cryptographic approval by N-of-M SOC owners.

📡

Cross-Device XDR (Sui Events)

Local heuristics learn about threats at lightning speed in a P2P model. A Lateral Movement attack blocked on server "A" automatically immunizes the entire organization's fleet in real time.

🔐

Homomorphic Encryption (SEAL)

Absolute privacy. Incident correlation occurs on encrypted telemetry using the Microsoft SEAL library. The SOC detects anomalies, but never reads your business secrets.

🪤

eBPF Deception Technology

Active attacker misdirection. Fake credentials and files (Honeypots/Honeytokens) injected directly by the OS kernel. The slightest interaction generates a 100% certain alert (zero false-positives).
